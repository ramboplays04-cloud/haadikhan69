HAADIKHAN69

Last updated: 13 November 2026

HAADIKHAN69 is a private tool used only by its owner to publish their own videos to Facebook Pages that they manage. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

What the app uses. When the owner connects Facebook, the app receives the list of Facebook Pages they manage and the access tokens needed to publish videos to those Pages. It uses the permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts and business_management only for this purpose.

How data is stored. Access tokens are encrypted with a password known only to the owner and are stored in the owner's own private storage. The app does not collect data about other Facebook users, Page visitors or followers.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party. Data is sent only to Facebook (Meta) to publish the owner's videos.

Contact. For any question about this policy, contact the app owner through the Facebook account that manages the connected Pages.

Terms of Service

This app is a personal tool. It may be used only by the owner and people they authorize, and only to publish content they own or have the rights to publish. Users must follow Facebook's Terms and Community Standards. The app is provided "as is" without any warranty.

Data Deletion Instructions

To delete all data this app holds about you:

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find HAADIKHAN69 and tap Remove. This immediately cancels all access.
  2. The app's saved tokens then stop working and are deleted the next time the owner clears the connection. You can also ask the owner to delete them at any time and it will be done within 7 days.
